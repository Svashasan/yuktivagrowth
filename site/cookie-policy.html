In short

  • Our hosting provider sets two strictly necessary cookies for security and load balancing.
  • Analytics and advertising tags are optional and listed below with their status.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, nothing optional runs until you consent.
  • You can change your choice at any time from Cookie settings in the footer.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local and session storage, do much the same job. They keep a site working, remember choices and measure how it is used.

How consent works here

Analytics and advertising tags are optional. We use Google Consent Mode v2 so that their default state depends on where you are:

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: everything optional is off until you choose, and no marketing tag fires before you consent.
  • Elsewhere, analytics and advertising tags that are in use may run by default, and you can reject them at any time with the cookie settings. [VERIFY: default for visitors outside the EEA, UK and Switzerland under UAE, KSA and Qatar law]

Strictly necessary items, such as the hosting cookies and the record of your choice, are always on.

Fonts and other requests

The site's fonts are hosted on our own server, so loading a page doesn't contact Google Fonts or any other third party. Links to LinkedIn, WhatsApp and Zoho Bookings take you to those services, which set their own cookies under their own policies.

Changing or withdrawing consent

  • Use the button below, or Cookie settings in the footer of any page, to change your choice at any time.
  • Withdrawing consent stops tags from running from then on. To remove cookies already set, clear them in your browser settings.
  • Google offers a browser add-on to opt out of Google Analytics on all sites.
