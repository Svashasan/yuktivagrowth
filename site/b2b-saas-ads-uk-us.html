What I'd run

  • LinkedIn audience cohorts by job function, seniority, company size and industry
  • Three-variant creative tests to find the message that lands with each role
  • Google Search campaigns on problem, category and alternative-to keywords
  • Demo and trial conversions tracked in GA4 and Tag Manager, behind consent for UK and EU visitors
  • A weekly budget review against cost per qualified lead

Relevant experience

For a B2B fintech business in India, I raised LinkedIn click-through rate 20%, grew monthly reach from about 8,000 to about 11,000 impressions and cut spend on low-converting ads by about 15% in eight weeks.
