LinkedIn Ads CTR up 20%

CredAble, Navi Mumbai. LinkedIn and Meta Ads. Customer Success Intern, May to August 2025.

Situation and objective

At CredAble I worked on paid campaigns on LinkedIn and Meta alongside my customer success role. The objective was to raise click-through rate and reach while spending less on ads that were not converting.

Starting point

Monthly reach was about 8K impressions on LinkedIn.

Strategy and prioritisation

Fix who the ads were shown to first, then test what they said. Budget decisions would follow CPL and ROAS rather than impressions alone.

Execution and notable decisions

Restructured the audience cohorts, ran three-variant creative tests, and reallocated Meta and LinkedIn budgets every week using CPL and ROAS signals.

Results, timeframe and measurement

LinkedIn Ads CTR rose 20% and monthly reach grew from about 8K to about 11K impressions. Low-converting ad spend fell by about 15% over eight weeks. Results were tracked on CTR, CPL and ROAS in MS Excel dashboards and MS PowerPoint decks for three stakeholders across 12 weekly review cycles.

Lessons and next steps

Audience structure and creative testing moved CTR, while weekly budget reviews kept cost per lead in check. I would take the same weekly CPL and ROAS routine into any account I manage.

Google and Meta Ads CTR up 20%

Awdiz Institute, Navi Mumbai. Google Ads and Meta Ads. Digital Marketing Intern, July to December 2023.

Situation and objective

As a digital marketing intern at Awdiz Institute I ran Google Ads and Meta Ads campaigns. The objective was to raise CTR and lower cost per lead.

Starting point

Campaigns were running on existing keyword match types and bidding settings, with no clear view of where visitors left the funnel.

Strategy and prioritisation

Tighten keyword targeting first, then use GA4 to find the weakest points in the funnel, then test creatives.

Execution and notable decisions

Rebuilt keyword match types and shifted to manual CPC bidding. Pinpointed three GA4 funnel drop-off points and A/B tested landing page fixes. Ran two to three creative variants per campaign and applied the top-converting formats to the next cycle.

Results, timeframe and measurement

CTR on Google and Meta Ads rose 20% over six weeks, and a further 12% from the creative testing. Cost per lead was lowered across two campaign cycles. I do not have a percentage for that reduction, so I have not quoted one.

Lessons and next steps

Match types and bidding control did the most for CTR, and the GA4 funnel view told me which landing page fixes were worth testing.

Property enquiries up 18%

Punit Enterprises, Navi Mumbai. Google Search and Meta. Digital Marketing Intern, April to May 2022.

Situation and objective

Punit Enterprises was advertising two real estate projects and wanted more property enquiries without raising the budget.

Starting point

The monthly ad budget was fixed at ₹25K across both active projects.

Strategy and prioritisation

Improve who saw the ads and what the ads said, rather than spend more.

Execution and notable decisions

Refined audience cohort filters and rewrote Google Search and Meta ad copy. Kept spend inside the ₹25K limit while lead volume grew week on week.

Results, timeframe and measurement

Property enquiries rose 18% across the two projects. This was a short engagement of about two months on a small budget, and I submitted monthly reports covering lead volume, spend and CPL trends.

Lessons and next steps

A fixed budget forces clear priorities. It was my first experience of reporting CPL trends to a business owner every month.
