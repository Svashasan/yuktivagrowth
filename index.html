How I run a campaign

Four stages, each with something you can check.

  1. 1

    Measure

    Conversion tracking checked or set up in GA4 and Tag Manager, and the funnel's drop-off points found, so every later decision has a number behind it.

  2. 2

    Structure

    Audience cohorts, keyword match types and ad copy built around who you want to reach and what they search for.

  3. 3

    Test

    Two to three creative variants per campaign and A/B tests on landing-page fixes. The winners carry into the next cycle.

  4. 4

    Report and reallocate

    Budget moves every week toward what brings leads at the lowest cost, and you get results in CPL, CPA and ROAS.

Which markets do you work with?

Businesses in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, the UK, the US and India. I work remotely from Navi Mumbai, India. India is 1 hour 30 minutes ahead of the UAE, 2 hours 30 minutes ahead of Saudi Arabia and Qatar, 4 hours 30 minutes to 5 hours 30 minutes ahead of the UK and 9 hours 30 minutes to 10 hours 30 minutes ahead of US Eastern Time.

Which platforms do you manage?

Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads for B2B, GA4, Tag Manager and conversion tracking, Email campaigns and customer retention. If you need a platform that isn't on this list, I'll tell you on the first call and suggest someone better placed.

How do you measure success?

On cost per lead (CPL), cost per acquisition (CPA) and return on ad spend (ROAS), measured as close to revenue as your data allows. Click-through rate and reach are useful for diagnosing problems, but they are not the goal.
